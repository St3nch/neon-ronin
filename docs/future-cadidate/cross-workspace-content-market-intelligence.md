## Human Authority Rule

Future agents may propose.

Future agents may draft.

Future agents may analyze.

Future agents may not:

- publish
- merge
- deploy
- approve their own work
- mark content final
- change pricing
- change service promises
- make business decisions
- execute external actions
- use customer/private data publicly
- turn one trend into an automatic workspace action

Human review remains required for consequential work.

## Candidate Workflow Later

Future candidate workflow:

```text
approved signal observed
-> evidence summarized
-> relevant workspace suggested
-> opportunity proposed
-> draft, outline, metadata, schema, or internal-link change prepared
-> human reviews claims, brand fit, privacy, evidence, and business value
-> approved work becomes a task or repo diff
-> build/check/preview runs where relevant
-> human approves merge or publication
-> deployment or publication occurs only through authorized workflow
-> audit/provenance record is kept
-> impact is monitored later if authorized
```

This workflow is not authorized now.
