# 00 - Origin And North Star

## Purpose

This document captures the original intent of Neon Ronin so the project does not drift into becoming a tool for only one business, one marketplace, or one workflow.

## Origin

The original inspiration was an app that ran agents to perform tasks for small businesses. One example was agents helping run a T-shirt design company on a marketplace.

That idea became the seed for Neon Ronin:

```text
Build an app that orchestrates agents to help run marketplace-style small businesses.
Do it better, smarter, safer, and more professionally.
```

Neon Ronin is not meant to be a single business.

Neon Ronin is meant to be the internal agent operating system that can support many small business ideas over time.

## Core Correction

One professional service business may be a real business that Neon Ronin supports.

A T-shirt marketplace business may be another business Neon Ronin supports.

A digital product business may be another.

A content business may be another.

An internal research workspace may be another.

None of these businesses are Neon Ronin itself.

They are workspaces inside Neon Ronin.

## North Star

```text
Neon Ronin is a human-controlled agent orchestration app for running and learning from multiple small businesses.
```

## What Neon Ronin Should Optimize For

Neon Ronin should optimize for:

- reusable workspace orchestration
- safe agent task execution
- human approval gates
- marketplace and business workflow support
- signal capture from real work
- shared intelligence through the Observatory
- clean separation between platform and business
- future expansion into new small business ideas

## What Neon Ronin Must Not Become

Neon Ronin must not become:

- a one-business backend
- a single service business app
- a single marketplace bot
- a single Etsy shop tool
- a report generator disguised as a platform
- a pile of business-specific workflows hardcoded into core

## Anti-Drift Rule

```text
When a business idea appears, Neon Ronin should ask:
What generic platform capability does this require?
```

It should not ask:

```text
How do we hardcode this business into Neon Ronin?
```

## Workspace Rule

```text
Businesses are tenants.
Neon Ronin is the building.
Agents are workers.
The Observatory is shared intelligence.
Humans make final decisions.
```

## Final Principle

```text
Neon Ronin exists to orchestrate agents across many small-business workspaces.
Any single business is only one use case.
```
