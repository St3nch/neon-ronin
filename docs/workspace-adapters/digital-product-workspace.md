# Digital Product Workspace Adapter

## Purpose

This adapter defines the generic pattern for a digital product business workspace.

It should not name a specific product brand.

## Typical Needs

- topic validation
- product packaging
- asset drafting
- launch preparation
- delivery preparation
- update/version tracking
- signal capture

## Typical Agents

| Agent | Role |
|---|---|
| Topic Research Agent | Evaluates demand and audience fit |
| Product Brief Agent | Structures the product concept |
| Asset Drafting Agent | Drafts supporting materials or assets |
| QA Agent | Checks quality, completeness, and consistency |
| Launch Prep Agent | Prepares launch or delivery packets |
| Signal Capture Agent | Submits generalized signals to the Observatory |

## Generic Workflow

```text
Human proposes product idea
-> Workspace validates topic or demand
-> Product brief is drafted
-> Assets are prepared
-> QA review occurs
-> Human approves launch packet
-> Product is manually released or scheduled through approved workflows
-> Generalized signals return to the Observatory
```

## Review Gates

- quality gate
- publish gate
- paid action gate
- rights and compliance gate

## Rule

```text
Digital product workspace agents may draft and organize.
They may not publish products or spend money without human approval.
```
