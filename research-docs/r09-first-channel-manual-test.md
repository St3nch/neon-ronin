# Neon Ronin r09 — First-Channel Manual Test Evaluation

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Best first channel candidate: Fiverr — productized Etsy SEO listing optimization service.** It uses the operator's actual edge (SEO), needs zero inventory, has a real buyer pool (Fiverr's Etsy-marketing subcategory shows 6,300–7,600+ active gigs, indicating substantive demand — Etsy SEO is not in Fiverr's top-10 oversaturated list dominated by logo design at ~185,000+ gigs), tolerates a 5–10 hr/week budget, and produces a fast yes/no signal (first order in 1–4 weeks for well-optimized gigs is typical; 1–3 months is the most cited range). Fulfillment is digital, IP risk is low (you optimize the buyer's listings, not your own), and Fiverr explicitly permits AI-assisted delivery with light disclosure requirements.
- **Best backup candidate: Etsy digital downloads in a defensible sub-niche** (e.g., B2B/realtor/Airbnb templates, niche SVG/clipart bundles for crafters, or D&D/TTRPG maps). Etsy provides built-in marketplace traffic that no other platform on the list matches for a no-audience operator (Etsy's 2025 10-K reports 86.5 million active buyers and 5.6 million active sellers as of December 31, 2025), and the SEO background applies directly. But Etsy carries the heaviest policy risk surface (see §4) and a $15 one-time new-shop verification fee plus identity verification (introduced 2024).
- **Worst candidate to start with: Amazon Merch on Demand.** Application gating (2–8 weeks typical, sometimes longer), Tier 10 cap, zero customer data, no margin lever, and the "10 designs until you hit 10 sales" feedback loop is too slow to validate anything inside 30 days.
- **Biggest surprise finding: Etsy's June 10, 2025 Creativity Standards update fundamentally narrowed POD on Etsy.** "Items must be produced based on a seller's original design" — purchased templates, licensed graphics, and template-based designs (even with commercial licenses) are now policy violations for "Made by a seller" classification. This makes generic Printify POD on Etsy materially riskier than it was 12 months ago for a fresh shop relying on AI-generated or purchased graphics. Enforcement has been aggressive: per Etsy's Q4 2023 earnings 8-K filed with the SEC, "In 2023, we removed approximately 140% more listings for violations of our Handmade Policy than in 2022" — and 2024–2025 community reports indicate the curve has steepened.
- **Biggest unresolved question: How quickly a Fiverr fresh account can actually rank for "Etsy SEO" specifically.** All "time to first order" data is anecdotal (Quora, BlackHatWorld, Medium); Fiverr publishes no official metric. A new gig may sit invisible for weeks regardless of quality.
- **Etsy verdict: Secondary, not first.** The platform has the strongest marketplace discovery for SEO-led sellers, but the combined load of (a) the $15 setup/verification fee, (b) ID verification, (c) the June 2025 originality crackdown, (d) automated AI-detection false positives reported throughout 2025, and (e) a new-shop "probation" cold-start makes it a worse first-test than a Fiverr service. Test Fiverr first; use the cash and signal to fund an Etsy digital-downloads experiment in week 3–4.

## 3. Channel Deep Dives

### Etsy POD (Printify)

**What we would sell:** AI-assisted or research-led niche designs on t-shirts, mugs, wall art via Printify integration.
**Why it could work:** Etsy reports 86.5 million active buyers as of December 31, 2025 (2025 10-K); Printify integration takes minutes; the SEO background gives a real listing-discovery edge.
**Why it could fail:** Three structural problems: (1) Etsy's June 10, 2025 update requires "items must be produced based on a seller's original design" — purchased templates and licensed POD graphics no longer qualify as "Made by a seller." (2) Aggressive automated AI-detection produces false-positive bans even on compliant sellers (XHBT 2025 policy guide: "Sellers using AI tools like Midjourney Pro with proper disclosure still face permanent bans"). (3) New shops now pay a one-time $15 verification fee (introduced 2024) and must complete ID verification before going live.
**Policy/platform risks:** High. Per Etsy's Q4 2023 earnings 8-K filed with the SEC: "In 2023, we removed approximately 140% more listings for violations of our Handmade Policy than in 2022"; community reports indicate 2024–2025 enforcement has intensified further.
**IP/common-sense risks:** Medium-high. AI-generated trademarks, accidental brand resemblance, training-data overlaps.
**Demand data sources:** eRank (free tier), EverBee (Chrome extension, 10–20 free searches/mo), Sale Samurai (paid), Etsy's own Marketplace Insights.
**Human work required:** Design generation/curation, mockup creation, listing copy, compliance review.
**Agent-assist opportunities:** Keyword research, listing drafts, mockup briefs.
**Manual test difficulty:** Medium-high — design pipeline + compliance review eats hours fast.
**Minimum viable tool stack and cost:** Printify free + Etsy ($15 verification + $0.20/listing) + Canva free + eRank free + ChatGPT/Claude ≈ **$15–$25 to launch**.
**Expected margin profile:** Per Printify's own model on a high-quality t-shirt: ~$26.70 net after Etsy fees from a $48 sale, minus $12.41 production + $3.99 shipping = ~$10 contribution margin. Healthy POD shops target 30–50% net margin.
**Verdict:** **Defer.** Too much policy risk for a 30-day cold-start test by an operator with an SEO background and no existing audience.

### Etsy digital downloads

**What we would sell:** PNG/SVG bundles, niche printables (B2B templates, realtor marketing kits, D&D maps, sublimation designs).
**Why it could work:** Instant fulfillment; SEO directly drives discovery; Etsy's algorithm rewards listing volume; bundled pricing ($5–$15) beats single files; categories like clipart sets, SVG cut files, and B2B templates show durable demand per multiple 2025–2026 trend reports (Outfy, MyDesigns, PODAutomation).
**Why it could fail:** Saturation is real (Accio: "concerns about market saturation are present among sellers"); generic AI-generated wall art is detectable and at risk; bundles need batch creation to hit algorithm velocity.
**Policy/platform risks:** Medium. June 2025 Creativity Standards removed prior language allowing digital scans of vintage content; "items must be produced based on a seller's original design" applies to digital files too. AI-generated work is allowed but must be disclosed and labeled "Designed by" (not "Made by").
**IP/common-sense risks:** Medium. Cricut/sublimation buyers are litigious about font/graphic licensing; commercial-use licenses must be airtight.
**Demand data sources:** eRank free, EverBee free tier (10–20 searches/mo), Etsy Marketplace Insights, Sale Samurai trial.
**Human work required:** Design curation + listing SEO + bundle composition.
**Agent-assist opportunities:** Keyword research, listing copy drafts, design-brief generation.
**Manual test difficulty:** Medium — first 30–50 listings is a real workload, but template-able.
**Minimum viable tool stack and cost:** Etsy ($15 verification, $0.20 × 20 listings = $4) + Canva free + eRank free + Claude/ChatGPT free ≈ **$20**.
**Expected margin profile:** ~85–90% gross margin per sale ($5 file − $0.20 listing − ~$0.50 transaction/processing fees).
**Verdict:** **Strong secondary candidate.** Test only after Fiverr proves out a feedback loop, OR run in parallel at 3–5 hr/week if the operator has bandwidth.

### Etsy personalized products

**What we would sell:** Name signs, custom prints, personalized POD via Customily/Printify personalization.
**Why it could work:** Highest-converting Etsy category; less AI-saturated; emotional gift purchases.
**Why it could fail:** Personalization means per-order manual review/approval. Customily and similar tools require setup; Printify's native personalization Hub link breaks on mobile and demands a "Review Needed" approval per order. Catastrophic time sink for 5–10 hr/week.
**Verdict:** **Defer.** Per-order human-in-loop kills the time budget.

### Gumroad

**What we could sell:** Notion templates, ebooks, SEO checklists, mini-courses.
**Why it could work:** 10-minute setup; zero approval; Gumroad Discover provides some traffic (one case study: 1,042 views → 121 sales mostly free downloads = $21.80 income). MoR handling.
**Why it could fail:** Discover is gated by lifetime sales thresholds for new sellers; one creator's 2025 stats: 7,068 product views all year = ~$25/mo income from Discover. Per Gumroad's official pricing page (verified May 2026), fees are now a flat **10% + $0.50 per transaction** for all direct sales regardless of lifetime volume, and **30%** on Discover marketplace sales — the old tiered volume-discount model was retired in 2023. One-year experiment summary: "Gumroad is not a marketplace that can give you organic traffic without work like Etsy. You need to send people to your Gumroad shop to get sales."
**Verdict:** **Use as cheap parallel test** if the operator has a one-page Notion or SEO template ready, but do not rely on it as the primary signal generator.

### Payhip

**What we would sell:** Same as Gumroad (digital products).
**Why it could work:** Free plan has all features; 5% transaction fee; built-in EU/UK VAT; instant payouts.
**Why it could fail:** Discovery marketplace is even thinner than Gumroad's; the platform is for people who already have traffic.
**Verdict:** **Defer.** Better economics than Gumroad, but no first-sale catalyst.

### Lemon Squeezy

**Why it could work:** Merchant of Record handles global tax; license keys; good for SaaS.
**Why it could fail:** Post-Stripe acquisition (2024), signup approval has slowed; sellers report more bans for restricted categories; pricing (5% + $0.50) doesn't beat Payhip; not optimized for printables/templates.
**Verdict:** **Defer.** Wrong tool for this test phase.

### Creative Market

**Why it could work:** Curated, 5M+ monthly visitors, high-trust buyers, designers willing to pay premium.
**Why it could fail:** Shop application requires 10–20 portfolio pieces, real review by a human curator, and many applicants are waitlisted ("not everyone gets their shop approved the first time"). Workaround: paid Dribbble Pro now grants access (Creative Market owns Dribbble). Even after approval, marketplace algorithm favors trendsetters.
**Verdict:** **Defer.** Too slow to start within 30 days unless the Dribbble Pro path is taken (~$80/year).

### Fiverr — Etsy SEO listing optimization

**What we would sell:** Productized Etsy SEO audits, title/tag/description optimization, shop setup, niche keyword research reports. Tiered: $20 (10 listings audit), $60 (30 listings full optimization), $150 (shop-wide SEO + keyword strategy doc).
**Why it could work:**
- Operator's SEO background is a direct multiplier here.
- Etsy SEO subcategory has 6,300–7,600+ gigs — substantive demand without being in the top-10 oversaturated zone (logo design ~185K gigs, AI art ~78K).
- Existing buyers explicitly become "Repeat Clients" — one gig review: "Repeat Client · United Kingdom · 5 · 2 months ago · The marketer worked hard to deliver and increased traffic alot in small time."
- Top Rated sellers (Kristen, 855 reviews; Subhan S., 333 reviews) charge $60–$120 entry; Vetted Pro Alan R. charges from $175. A new seller can enter at $10–$25 starter packages and still see real order volumes ($50–$200 per order is common per gig reviews).
- Fiverr's AI policy explicitly permits AI-assisted delivery: "Originality: Freelancers should provide customized, original work for each client and avoid reusing or mass-producing AI-generated content."
- Cash + signal in same loop: each order is direct market validation that someone with money pays for "AI-assisted Etsy SEO research."

**Why it could fail:**
- "Time to first order" data is entirely anecdotal. Multiple Quora and Medium sources suggest 1–3 months is common; one BHW seller: "Just started my Fiverr gig for translation and SEO copywriting. The no-review phase is brutal." A new gig may sit invisible for weeks.
- Fiverr's algorithm in 2025 reportedly favors paid ads and off-platform authority over organic ranking ("Fiverr's 2025 algorithm prioritizes paid ads and off-platform authority over organic rankings, with gig positions fluctuating daily").
- Buyer-detected AI delivery causes negative reviews: one Etsy SEO gig reviewer wrote "The work felt AI-generated rather than manually researched, which is not what I was looking for."
- Tizzit (Etsy educator) cautionary tale: a buyer hired a cheap Fiverr Etsy SEO optimizer who "deleted the titles and tags that were bringing people to her shop, but used titles and tags that were way too broad… She lost a lot of traffic, her sales went way down."

**Policy/platform risks:** Low. No bulk-output policy beyond originality. Account suspension risk only for IP/illegal content, not for legitimate SEO work.
**IP/common-sense risks:** Very low — you are optimizing the buyer's listings, not creating IP. The biggest reputational risk is bad output destroying a buyer's shop (mitigated by human review).
**Demand data sources:** Fiverr search counters per subcategory; "Briefs" matching; competitor gig reviews (sorted by recency) reveal what is being ordered weekly.
**Human work required:** Initial gig setup (one weekend), per-order research + delivery (1–3 hr/order), buyer comms.
**Agent-assist opportunities:** Keyword research via Claude/ChatGPT + eRank free, listing draft generation, audit report templating, competitive analysis.
**Manual test difficulty:** Low-medium. Hardest part is the no-review cold start.
**Minimum viable tool stack and cost:** Fiverr (free) + eRank free + Canva free + Claude/ChatGPT free + Google Sheets ≈ **$0 to launch**.
**Expected margin profile:** Fiverr takes 20%. On a $50 order: ~$40 net before time cost. At 2 hr/order, that's $20/hr effective. Higher-tier orders ($100–$200) push effective to $40–$80/hr.
**Verdict:** **Primary recommendation.** Highest fit on the scorecard, lowest setup friction, lowest IP risk, best SEO leverage, and directly converts the operator's existing skill into validated buyer demand.

### Fiverr — YouTube thumbnails / creator services

**Why it could work:** High demand; creators buy weekly; clear bundle pricing.
**Why it could fail:** Saturated (top-10 oversaturated list); doesn't use SEO background; design-quality bar is high; doesn't generate Neon Ronin–relevant market intelligence (the project is about commerce research, not creator design).
**Verdict:** **Defer.** Off-thesis for Neon Ronin's positioning.

### Fiverr — productized creative/SEO services generally

**Verdict:** Equivalent to #8. The "general productized SEO" framing is fine but the Etsy-specific angle is more defensible because it (a) ties to a marketplace the operator can deeply understand, (b) has more concrete repeat-buyer signal, (c) directly feeds future Neon Ronin agent-assisted research products.

### Shopify / direct website

**Why it could fail:** Shopify Basic is $39/month billed monthly (or $29/month billed annually, per Shopify's official pricing page). Zero traffic without external marketing. Discovery requires SEO that takes months. Not appropriate for a 30-day manual validation test.
**Verdict:** **Defer.**

### TikTok Shop

**Why it could work:** 24–48 hr approval; new-seller 3% referral fee promo for 30 days after first sale (effective April 1, 2025); strong overall platform momentum — per Momentum Works & Tabcut's "TikTok Shop in the U.S. H1 2025" report, "In the first half of 2025, TikTok Shop's global GMV reached US$26.2 billion, doubling year-on-year."
**Why it could fail:** Requires daily video content (operator has 5–10 hr/week); 24–48 hr shipping standard for physical goods; "shoppable content video posting limits" introduced May 11, 2026 for US merchants; Shop Performance Score gating; digital-only sellers face high friction.
**Verdict:** **Defer.** Time-budget mismatch.

### Amazon Merch on Demand

**Why it could fail:** Application approval cited at 2–8 weeks (some 2 weeks to 3 months); rejection emails opaque; Tier 10 cap requires 10 sales before unlocking 25 designs (loop too slow for 30-day test); royalty math (~$1.93 on a $19.99 tee at Tier 10 per one seller report); zero customer data.
**Verdict:** **Defer.** Possibly revisit at month 3+ as a passive design-validation channel after Etsy/Fiverr generate concrete winning concepts.

### Amazon Handmade

**Why it could fail:** Strict "handmade only by you or ≤20 employees" rule; application review 7–14 days; $39.99/mo Professional plan required (waived after approval starting month 2); no digital products, no POD. Fundamentally incompatible with an AI-assisted commerce research project.
**Verdict:** **Not at all.**

### Redbubble / Society6 / TeePublic / Threadless

**Why it could work:** Easy account setup; built-in marketplace.
**Why it could fail:** Redbubble explicitly caps new accounts at 5 AI-tagged uploads/day, 20/day for established sellers (100+ sales). Royalties ~20%. Marketplace flooded; one seller acknowledged: "30 designs across all platforms" cap was introduced to reduce AI spam. Society6 and Teespring have similar restrictions. No SEO leverage — these are tag-stuffing platforms.
**Verdict:** **Defer.** Maybe a tertiary parallel test in month 2+.

### Creative Fabrica / Design Bundles / Envato / Canva templates

**Why it could fail:** Each requires application + curation; Envato and Canva templates have specific portfolio requirements; Canva Creator program is invite/curated; ramp time exceeds 30 days.
**Verdict:** **Defer.**

### Notion template marketplaces

**Why it could work:** Lowest friction; Gumroad is the dominant Notion template marketplace; can be tested in parallel for $0.
**Why it could fail:** Requires existing audience (Twitter/X, Reddit, blog) for traffic. Notion Marketplace itself accepts free templates as a discovery channel but monetization happens off-Notion. Discovery is the bottleneck.
**Verdict:** **Test in parallel as a cheap experiment** — but not the primary.

## 8. Manual Validation Criteria (30-Day Targets)

| Metric | Target | Kill criterion | Continue criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| First sale timing | ≤ 21 days | 30 days, zero orders, zero meaningful inquiries → kill | 1+ order in 21 days |
| Gross revenue | $150–$500 in 30 days | < $50 gross and no inbound inquiries → kill | $150+ |
| Net margin per order (Fiverr) | ≥ 60% (after 20% Fiverr cut + time) | < 30% effective | ≥ 50% |
| Time per delivered order | ≤ 2.5 hr | > 4 hr/order consistently | ≤ 2.5 hr |
| Human review minutes per order | ≤ 20 min | > 60 min | ≤ 30 min |
| Repeat-buyer signal | 1+ repeat order or 1+ explicit "I'd come back" | Zero across 5+ completed orders | 1+ in 30 days |
| Inbound inquiries (gig clicks/messages) | ≥ 15 inquiries / 30 days | < 5 | ≥ 10 |
| Negative review / dispute rate | 0 | Any account warning | 0 |

**Kill criteria (any one triggers reassessment):**
1. Zero orders and < 5 inbound inquiries by day 30.
2. First completed order generates negative review or platform warning.
3. Effective hourly rate < $15/hr across 5+ orders.
4. Fiverr algorithm visibility drops to zero (impressions < 10/week).

**Continue criteria (all must be met):**
1. 1+ paid order within 21 days.
2. ≥ $150 gross by day 30.
3. ≥ 1 buyer signals repeat intent OR ≥ 10 gig inquiries.

## 9. Best First Manual Experiment

**Channel:** Fiverr — "Etsy SEO listing optimization" productized service.

**Offer/product type (tiered packages):**
- **Starter ($20):** 10-listing keyword + title audit, delivered as Google Doc within 3 days.
- **Standard ($60):** 30-listing full optimization (titles, tags, descriptions, alt text) within 5 days.
- **Pro ($150):** Shop-wide SEO + 1-page keyword strategy doc + 60-day check-in offer.

Plus a second gig — "Marketplace listing audit (Etsy / Shopify)" — to hedge first-order timing.

**Why this one:**
1. Maximum leverage of the operator's existing SEO skill (the only durable edge they have on day 1).
2. Zero IP risk — the operator does not create or sell any product themselves.
3. Cash + signal in one loop — each order is paying market validation that "AI-assisted Etsy research" has commercial demand.
4. Lowest setup friction of any channel: $0 to launch, no approval gate, no inventory, no ID verification beyond basic Fiverr signup.
5. Directly feeds Neon Ronin's thesis: every order is a real buyer telling you what they want optimized, which is primary research data for the agent-assisted commerce system.

**Manual workflow:**
1. Day 1–3: Create Fiverr account; build 2–3 gigs (Etsy SEO + Marketplace Audit + Pinterest-for-Etsy); craft gig images in Canva; draft a delivery template Google Doc.
2. Day 4–7: Publish gigs; respond to all "Briefs" matches within 1 hour; promote on Reddit (r/Etsy, r/EtsySellers — within rules) and LinkedIn.
3. Day 8–30: Per-order workflow: (a) buyer sends listings → (b) Claude/ChatGPT does first-pass keyword research → (c) eRank validates volumes → (d) operator reviews and approves → (e) deliver Google Doc + Loom video walkthrough → (f) ask for review.
4. Daily 5–10 min: check messages and Briefs.
5. Weekly: log every order's industry, buyer pain point, listing types — this is the Neon Ronin research substrate.

**Data sources to use:** eRank (free), EverBee (free tier 10–20 searches/mo), Etsy public search (manual research), Google Trends, Claude/ChatGPT for drafts.

**Human review gates:**
- All keyword suggestions reviewed before delivery (no raw AI output).
- All listing copy edited for tone (Etsy listings need warmth; pure AI flattens this).
- Delivery doc reviewed for accuracy + compliance (no trademark suggestions).

**Minimum tool stack and cost:** Fiverr (free) + eRank free + Canva free + ChatGPT/Claude free + Google Workspace (free) + Loom (free) = **$0**.

**Success criteria:** Hit continue criteria in §8.

**Failure criteria:** Hit kill criteria in §8.

**Risks:**
1. Cold-start invisibility — gig sits unseen 2–4 weeks. *Mitigation:* 2–3 gigs across adjacent niches; LinkedIn/Reddit outreach.
2. AI-detected delivery → bad first review. *Mitigation:* Human-review gate is non-negotiable; deliver Loom walkthrough that visibly shows human reasoning.
3. Algorithm change devalues organic ranking. *Mitigation:* Plan to test $10–$20 Fiverr Ads spend in week 3 if zero orders by day 14.
4. Buyer's Etsy shop gets harmed by your advice (Tizzit anecdote risk). *Mitigation:* No destructive changes (no tag deletion without buyer confirmation); deliver a "review before applying" doc, not a done-for-you edit.

**What this teaches Neon Ronin:**
- Real demand signal: which Etsy verticals are buying SEO help right now.
- Buyer language: how non-SEO buyers describe their problems — direct prompt-engineering input.
- Pricing tolerance: what people pay for "AI-assisted commerce research."
- Workflow bottlenecks: where agents actually save time vs. where human judgment matters.
- A first paying customer cohort that can later test a productized Neon Ronin software offering.

## 13. Final Recommendation

- **Primary channel to test first:** Fiverr — productized Etsy SEO listing optimization (with a second hedge gig: marketplace listing audit). Run for 30 days.
- **Secondary channel (start week 3 if primary is producing signal, or sooner if bandwidth allows):** Etsy digital downloads in a defensible niche (B2B/realtor templates, niche SVG bundles, or a single targeted printable category) — 5 listings in 30 days.
- **Cheap parallel test (optional, ~1 hr/week):** One Notion or SEO template on Gumroad. Zero cost, tests no-marketplace distribution.
- **Channels to defer:** Etsy POD, Amazon Merch, Amazon Handmade, Redbubble/Society6/TeePublic/Threadless/Zazzle/Spring, Shopify direct, TikTok Shop, Lemon Squeezy, Payhip, Creative Market, Creative Fabrica/Design Bundles/Envato/Canva templates.
- **Channel to not test at all:** Amazon Handmade (incompatible with AI-assisted thesis).

**Biggest unknowns:**
1. How long a fresh Fiverr account takes to surface in Etsy SEO search. All data is anecdotal; the operator may sit invisible for 2–4 weeks before order #1.
2. Whether AI-assisted SEO delivery quality survives buyer scrutiny in 2026 — Fiverr's AI policy is permissive, but buyers actively flag AI-tasting output.
3. Whether Etsy's automated detection will tighten further against AI-assisted *listings* (which would harm the secondary Etsy digital-downloads test) before the test concludes.

**What data to gather during the manual test:**
- Per-order: buyer's industry/niche, listing count, pain point, hourly time spent, gross revenue, repeat intent, review.
- Per-week: gig impressions, clicks, conversion rate, inbound message volume.
- Per-30-days: total revenue, effective hourly rate, repeat-buyer count, NPS-style "would they refer" signal.

**Recommended next research topic:** "Demand mapping for AI-assisted commerce-research as a productized service" — once the first 10 Fiverr orders are complete, the next research brief should map *which buyer segments* are paying the most for SEO/commerce research help (Etsy sellers, Shopify DTC, Amazon FBA, Pinterest creators), what they currently spend, and which sub-segments would tolerate a $99–$299/mo productized agent-assisted offering. That answer determines whether Neon Ronin's eventual software product targets Etsy sellers specifically or marketplace sellers broadly.

### Notes on source quality and skepticism

- Etsy buyer/seller counts (86.5M buyers, 5.6M sellers as of Dec 31, 2025) are from Etsy's 2025 Annual Report 10-K filed with the SEC — a primary source.
- The "140% more listings removed for Handmade Policy violations" figure is from Etsy's Q4 2023 earnings 8-K (a primary SEC filing). The "4×" figure that has circulated in secondary articles is not corroborated in primary filings; the auditable number is 2.4×.
- Etsy policy quotes are taken from Etsy's own legal/seller-handbook pages and corroborated by independent recaps (Marmalead, eRank, ecombalance, XHBT).
- Fiverr "time to first order" data is *entirely* anecdotal — Quora, BlackHatWorld, Medium. No platform-published metric exists. Stated 1–3 month range is directional only.
- Fiverr gig-count figures (6,300+ / 7,600+ in Etsy subcategories) come from Fiverr's own filtered-category result counters surfaced via search; not an officially published statistic.
- Gumroad's flat 10% + $0.50 fee structure (verified via Gumroad's official pricing page, May 2026) replaced the older tiered volume-discount model in 2023; older articles still citing the sliding scale are outdated.
- The Etsy new-shop verification fee is a one-time $15 charge (introduced 2024). Some third-party blogs cite a "$15–$29 range," but Etsy's own communications and SEC filings document a $15 single-tier fee.
- POD margin examples (Printify's ~$10 contribution on a $48 t-shirt) come from Printify's own published calculator and should be treated as illustrative, not promise.
- TikTok Shop GMV ($26.2B H1 2025) comes from Momentum Works & Tabcut's "TikTok Shop in the U.S. H1 2025" report — a third-party market research firm, not a TikTok earnings filing.
- One specific cautionary tale (Tizzit: a buyer's Etsy shop harmed by cheap Fiverr SEO help) is a single anecdote from an Etsy educator with a competing interest. Treat as directional only — mitigation built into the recommendation.
