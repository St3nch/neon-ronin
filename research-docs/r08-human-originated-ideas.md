# r08 — Human-Originated Ideas as First-Class Inputs

## TL;DR

Neon Ronin should not treat agents as the sole source of product, service, or business ideas.

Human-originated ideas are first-class inputs.

Core principle:

> **Your ideas + agent research + human approval.**

Agents can discover, validate, expand, organize, and draft. But the human operator can originate ideas directly, decide which ideas matter, and determine what moves forward.

This is important because Neon Ronin is not meant to replace the operator's taste, SEO judgment, niche instincts, or creative direction. It is meant to amplify them.

---

## 1. Why This Matters

A fully agent-generated commerce system creates several problems:

- ideas can become generic
- agents may chase demand signals without taste
- agents may overfit to saturated trends
- agents may generate IP-risky ideas
- agents may produce marketplace slop
- human creativity becomes an afterthought

Neon Ronin should work differently.

The human operator brings:

- SEO experience
- niche instincts
- taste
- business judgment
- common sense
- creative direction
- understanding of what feels worth pursuing
- ability to reject ideas that look profitable but dumb, risky, or low-quality

Agents should support that judgment, not replace it.

---

## 2. Core Principle

```text
Human ideas are first-class inputs.
Agents assist, validate, expand, and prepare.
The human chooses what moves forward.
```

Short version:

```text
Agents discover and propose.
Humans originate and decide.
```

This means Neon Ronin needs two idea paths:

1. Human-originated ideas
2. Agent-discovered opportunities

Both paths eventually go through human review.

---

## 3. Human-Originated Idea Workflow

When the operator has an idea, the system should support it directly.

```text
Human enters idea
→ Agent researches demand
→ Agent finds keyword/market evidence
→ Agent suggests variants and angles
→ Agent surfaces possible concerns
→ Agent prepares review packet
→ Human decides whether to move forward
```

Example human-originated ideas:

```text
retirement candles for nurses
funny pickleball gifts
custom pet memorial templates
SEO service for Etsy sellers
wedding sign printables
custom family recipe binder template
teacher appreciation mugs
Notion templates for freelancers
Canva templates for local service businesses
```

The agent's job is not to say, "This is approved."

The agent's job is to answer:

```text
Is there demand?
What keywords support it?
What variants exist?
What competitors are ranking?
What channels fit best?
What are safer angles?
What personalization hooks work?
What are the obvious risks?
What would be a good first test?
```

---

## 4. Agent-Discovered Opportunity Workflow

Agents may also discover opportunities from approved data sources.

```text
Agent finds demand signal
→ Agent proposes idea
→ Agent explains supporting evidence
→ Human approves / edits / rejects / parks
→ Only approved ideas move forward
```

This path is useful for:

- keyword gaps
- seasonal trends
- underserved niches
- competitor pattern discovery
- product/service angle discovery
- long-tail buyer intent
- cross-platform demand triangulation

But agent-discovered does not mean agent-approved.

---

## 5. Idea Intake Should Support Both Paths

Neon Ronin should have an idea intake layer that can accept:

| Input Type | Source | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Human idea** | Operator manually enters it. | "Custom pet memorial ornament for cats." |
| **Keyword seed** | Operator enters keyword. | "retirement gift nurse." |
| **Niche seed** | Operator enters niche. | "pickleball players over 50." |
| **Product seed** | Operator enters product type. | "candles," "printables," "Notion templates." |
| **Audience seed** | Operator enters target buyer or recipient. | "new moms," "teachers," "realtors." |
| **Agent opportunity** | Agent finds signal from approved data. | Rising Google/Pinterest trend. |
| **Competitor pattern** | Agent notices repeated marketplace pattern. | Personalized profession gifts ranking often. |
| **Service idea** | Human or agent proposes a Fiverr/productized-service offer. | Etsy SEO listing rewrite package. |

Every idea should be traceable to its origin.

---

## 6. Idea Source Metadata

Each idea should carry metadata about where it came from.

Useful metadata:

- idea source: human / agent / imported / hybrid
- source note
- source keyword
- source platform
- source data provider
- created timestamp
- creator: human or agent name/job
- current status
- human decision
- human notes
- linked research packet
- linked review packet

Example:

```text
Idea: Personalized retirement candle for nurses
Source: Human
Source note: Operator suggested this from prior SEO/niche intuition.
Agent task: Validate demand and find safer keyword variants.
Status: Under review
Human decision: Pending
```

This matters because the project should be able to show when the operator originated or shaped an idea.

---

## 7. Relationship to Etsy / Marketplace Compliance

Human-originated ideas help strengthen the project's posture for marketplace compliance, especially on platforms like Etsy.

Benefits:

| Area | Why human-originated ideas help |
|---|---|
| **Originality** | Ideas are not purely model-generated from scraped trends. |
| **Seller involvement** | The operator is visibly making creative and business decisions. |
| **IP/common-sense review** | The human can reject risky concepts early. |
| **Quality** | Human taste filters out generic AI output. |
| **Differentiation** | The operator's SEO and niche instincts become part of the edge. |
| **Audit trail** | The system can preserve human idea source, edits, notes, and approvals. |

This does not eliminate Etsy risk.

It does make the system more defensible than a fully autonomous AI product generator.

---

## 8. Idea Statuses

Ideas should move through clear statuses.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Inbox** | Captured but not reviewed. |
| **Researching** | Agent is gathering demand/context. |
| **Ready for Review** | Review packet is prepared. |
| **Approved** | Human approved moving forward. |
| **Edited** | Human revised the concept. |
| **Parked** | Keep for later. |
| **Rejected** | Do not pursue. |
| **Needs Legal Review** | IP/legal uncertainty too high. |
| **Needs More Data** | Insufficient demand or competitive evidence. |
| **Converted to Project** | Moved into design/listing/service workflow. |

Agents can suggest a next status, but humans decide status changes that move ideas forward.

---

## 9. Human Decision Types

When reviewing an idea, the human should have simple decision options.

| Decision | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Approve** | Move into next workflow. |
| **Edit** | Change concept, keyword, audience, product type, or angle. |
| **Reject** | Do not pursue. |
| **Park** | Save for later. |
| **Ask Agent to Research More** | Need more demand, competitor, or channel evidence. |
| **Ask Agent for Variants** | Generate safer or more specific alternatives. |
| **Mark as Risky** | Possible IP/platform/common-sense concern. |
| **Needs Legal Review** | Do not continue without expert review. |

The system should make rejection and parking easy. Not every idea deserves oxygen.

---

## 10. What Agents Should Do With Human Ideas

For a human-submitted idea, agents can help by producing:

- keyword expansion
- buyer-intent analysis
- channel suggestions
- competitor examples
- product variants
- personalization hooks
- seasonal timing notes
- price/margin assumptions
- possible marketplace risks
- possible IP/common-sense concerns
- safer alternatives
- first-test suggestions
- draft listing angles
- draft design prompt options

Agents should avoid turning a human idea into something totally different unless asked.

The human idea should remain the anchor.

---

## 11. What Agents Should Not Do With Human Ideas

Agents should not:

- override the human's intent
- declare the idea approved
- automatically create marketplace listings
- automatically generate a full product line
- automatically spend heavily on data/design generation
- push the idea into production without review
- transform it into brand-adjacent or IP-risky variants
- bury the original human idea under dozens of unrelated AI suggestions

The system should avoid "idea drift."

---

## 12. Review Packet for Human-Originated Ideas

A good review packet should include:

- original human idea
- interpreted meaning
- suggested niche/category
- buyer/persona hypothesis
- target platform options
- keyword evidence
- trend evidence
- competitor observations
- product/service variants
- personalization hooks
- estimated difficulty
- estimated margin potential
- possible concerns
- safer alternatives
- suggested next step

Example:

```text
Original idea:
Personalized retirement candles for nurses

Agent summary:
This appears to target gift buyers looking for profession-specific retirement gifts. Strong possible personalization hooks include name, retirement date, hospital/department nickname, and message customization.

Evidence to gather:
- Google demand for "nurse retirement gift"
- Etsy listing count and top listing patterns
- Pinterest trend curve for retirement gift searches
- competitor pricing range

Possible concerns:
- Avoid hospital logos, medical organization marks, and copyrighted quotes.
- Avoid making medical claims.

Suggested next step:
Research 20 keyword variants and compare Etsy digital/POD/candle opportunities.
```

---

## 13. Relationship to Managed Agent Runtime

This principle fits r07's managed agent runtime.

Human-originated ideas can trigger controlled work:

```text
Human enters idea
→ On-demand research job starts
→ Budget and source limits apply
→ Agent prepares packet
→ Item appears in review queue
→ Human decides
```

The agent does not need to run endlessly.

It can run when asked, on schedule, or within a watch mode that the human controls.

---

## 14. Planning Implications

When official planning docs are created, they should preserve these requirements:

1. Idea intake must support human-originated ideas.
2. Human ideas should be traceable as human-originated.
3. Agents should validate and expand ideas, not replace them.
4. Agent-discovered ideas require human approval before moving forward.
5. Human-originated ideas also require human approval before design/listing/publish steps.
6. The review queue should show both human-originated and agent-originated items.
7. The system should record human edits, approvals, rejections, and notes.
8. No idea should become a product solely because an agent scored it highly.

---

## 15. Final Principle

```text
Neon Ronin is not AI pretending to be the founder.

It is the operator's strategy, taste, and SEO judgment amplified by controlled agents.
```

Short version:

> **Human ideas are the spark. Agents bring fuel, maps, and scouting reports. The human chooses the fire.**
